# Community Civic Issue & Waste Management System

## 1. Title
Community Civic Issue & Waste Management System

## 2. Domain
Civic Services & Waste Management

## 3. Who is the User?
- Resident – Reports and tracks civic issues.
- Municipal Staff – Views and resolves assigned complaints.
- Admin – Manages users, complaints, departments and assignments.

## 4. What Problem Are We Solving?
Residents face issues such as garbage accumulation, drainage blockages and other civic problems in their locality. They often do not know the correct department to contact or how to track their complaints. This can result in delayed reporting and resolution. The system provides a centralized platform to report and track civic issues.

## 5. Proposed Solution
The application allows residents to:
- Report civic issues with photos and location.
- Select the issue category and provide a description.
- Track complaint status.
- Route complaints to the appropriate department.
- Allow municipal staff to update the resolution status.

## 6. Core Entities / Database Tables
- User
- Complaint
- Department
- Staff
- ComplaintImage

## 7. User Roles & Permissions
- Resident – Create and track complaints.
- Municipal Staff – View assigned complaints and update status.
- Admin – Manage users, departments, complaints and staff.

## 8. Success Criteria
- Users can submit complaints with photo, location and description.
- Complaints are assigned to the appropriate department.
- Users can track complaints until resolution.

## 9. Out of Scope
- Online payment processing
- Emergency response services
- IoT-based smart waste-bin monitoring
- Real-time GPS tracking of municipal vehicles

## 10. Chosen Track
Java (Spring Boot)
## Project Scope

The system enables residents to report local civic issues such as waste accumulation, drainage blockages and sanitation problems. Complaints are recorded with location and image details and routed to the appropriate municipal department for resolution.

## Key Features

- Civic issue reporting
- Photo and location upload
- Complaint tracking
- Department-based assignment
- Complaint status updates
